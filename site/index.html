Everything at a glance, nothing in the way

No sign-in

Reads the logins Claude Code and Codex already saved. Nothing to set up.

Windows + WSL

Finds your accounts in your Windows profile and in every WSL distro.

Right in your taskbar

A small widget next to the clock. Click it for every account, right-click for more. It follows the taskbar's light or dark theme and steps aside for fullscreen apps.

Multiple accounts

Add every Claude and Codex account and switch with one click, or automatically at your limit.

Limit resets

See the usage-limit resets each account holds, and when they expire.

Speaks your language

Ten languages, following Windows. Signed auto-updates keep it current.

More accounts, fewer walls

Add accounts with the CLI's own sign-in, in an isolated folder, so your current login is never touched. Switch with a click from the flyout.

Turn on Switch automatically and SlopBar moves to the account with the most headroom when the active one reaches your threshold. The official CLI keeps doing all the work; nothing is proxied.

Your data stays yours

What it reads

The login files of Claude Code (~/.claude) and Codex (~/.codex) on Windows and in WSL. Saved accounts live in a folder on your PC, just like the CLIs keep them.

Who it talks to

Only Anthropic and OpenAI, to read your usage — and GitHub, to check for updates. No analytics, no servers of its own.

Open source

Every line is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0. Read it, build it yourself, or help make it better.

Get started in a minute

  1. 1
    Download the installer from the latest release.
  2. 2
    Run it. SlopBar appears in your taskbar, next to the clock.
  3. 3
    That's it. If you use Claude Code or Codex, your usage is already there.
